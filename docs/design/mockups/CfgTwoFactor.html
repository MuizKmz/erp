Set up two-factor sign-in

Your role (Administrator) handles admin and finance work, so a code from your phone is needed at each sign-in.

  1. Open an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator …).
  2. Scan the code, or type this key: JBSW Y3DP EHPK 3PXP
  3. Type the 6-digit code the app shows.
Turn on two-factor
Next you get 8 recovery codes. Keep them safe: each one signs you in once if you lose your phone. The Administrator can also reset your two-factor.
