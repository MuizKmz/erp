Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Create invoice from orders
S18 · Create invoice
Opens Invoice and clicks Create invoice. Step 1 lists orders with delivered but uninvoiced quantity; step 2 sets quantities; step 3 reviews the draft.Only delivered quantity can be invoiced. Invoice now must be between 1 and delivered minus already invoiced.Draft invoice, linked to the order and the DO.
2
Price below list or customer over limit?
—
The screen compares each price with the price list and checks the customer’s credit.Price below the list, or over the limit, needs approval. Rules from Credit Control (S29).—
3
Approve the invoice
S02 · Approvals inbox
Approver sees the line price against the price list, or the credit exposure, and decides.Sales Manager decides on price; Finance Manager on credit.Approved: can be released. Rejected: back to draft.
4
Release the invoice
S19 · Invoice
Clicks Release.A released invoice can never be edited or deleted. Corrections use a debit note or a sales return.Released · Unpaid, due date from the credit term.
5
Print or email it
S20 · Print previewS33 · Email templates
Print opens the A4 preview. Email uses the invoice template with the PDF attached.Company details, bank account and logo come from Master Data.The customer has the invoice.
6
Posting queued for Finance; e-Invoice queued for LHDN
—
Two banners: Accounting posting: queued and LHDN e-Invoice: pending.The e-Invoice is never skipped. It is submitted when the e-Invoice module is plugged in.Once connected: receivable posted, e-Invoice validated, QR and UUID printed.
7
Invoice correct?
—
Customer checks quantity, price and condition.—Wrong, damaged or missed charges go to F6.
8
Pays by the due date
—
Customer pays by transfer or cheque.——
9
Finance records the payment
—
Receipts are recorded in Finance and show on the invoice under Payments received.Sales never changes a balance by hand.Balance due 0, Paid. Overdue invoices feed the credit check in F2.
