Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
What happened?
P03 · Document flow
Buyer opens the PO’s Document flow to see ordered, received, billed and returned.Goods not received cannot be returned; goods received cannot be cancelled.One of three paths.
2
Draft a cancellation for the balance
P12 · Cancel order listP13 · Cancel PO balance
From Cancel Order, or Cancel balance on the PO. Enters the cancel qty, reason and the supplier’s letter.Only the part not yet received can be cancelled.PCO number, Draft.
3
Release the cancellation
P13 · Cancel PO balance
Purchasing Manager reviews and releases.Permission purchase.cancel_order.release. Buyers can only draft.Released.
4
PO closes; committed spend drops
—
Nothing to click.Committed spend drops by the cancelled value.PO Done · balance cancelled. If still needed, the item shows again on the demand list.
5
Create a return from the goods receipt
P22 · Return listP23 · Purchase return
From Purchase Return. Picks the goods receipt, return qty, reason, settle by (credit note or replacement) and the RMA; adds photos.Return qty can never be more than received minus earlier returns.PRT number, Draft.
6
Hand the goods to the supplier
—
Store hands the goods to the supplier’s lorry, then the return is released.—Return notice emailed with the RMA (P26).
7
Stock-out saved; credit note expected
—
A banner says Inventory is not connected yet.The stock-out event waits for Inventory.The return shows under “credit notes to chase”.
8
Record the supplier’s credit note
—
AP records the supplier’s credit note against the return.The credit note must point to the return.What we owe drops (PI-0001: RM 4,180.00 → 3,740.00).
9
Record the supplier debit note
P20 · Debit note listP21 · Supplier debit note
From Debit Note. Picks the supplier invoice, enters their debit note number and the charge.A debit note always points to one supplier invoice. Services use the service tax code.PDN number, Draft.
10
Charge on the PO?
—
The screen checks the charge against the PO and quotation.Charges not on the PO need approval (P24).—
11
Approve the extra charge
P02 · Approvals inbox
Manager sees the check result and the supplier’s explanation in Approvals.—Approved or rejected with a comment.
12
We owe more; posting queued
—
Nothing to click.Posting waits for Finance.We owe more on that invoice (PI-0002: + RM 540.00).
