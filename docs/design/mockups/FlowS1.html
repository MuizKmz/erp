Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Record the enquiry
S07 · New enquiry
Clicks Add new enquiry. Picks the customer from Master Data; a read-only card shows credit limit, amount owed and price list. Or ticks New prospect. Adds type, source and items of interest.Enquiry type and source come from Enquiry Types (S31). Budget and needed-by date are optional.Number EQ-2609-0011 on save. Save and forward notifies the agent: New → Forwarded.
2
Customer already in Master Data?
—
Nothing to do if the customer exists. A prospect is flagged on the enquiry.A quotation needs a customer from Master Data. Sales cannot create customers.Admin adds the customer in Core; the enquiry keeps its link.
4
Create the quotation
S09 · QuotationS04 · Add item
Clicks Create quotation. Customer and items are copied. Add item shows price list, last price to this customer and stock at the branch.Excluded items (S32) and items not for sale cannot be added. Over the credit limit only warns on a quotation.QT number, revision 0, Draft. Enquiry → Quoted.
5
Discount above 15%?
—
Types a discount per line. Estimated margin is visible to the manager only.A discount above 15% needs approval before release.—
6
Approve the discount
S02 · Approvals inbox
Manager opens the item in Approvals, sees margin and the agent’s reason, and approves or rejects with a comment.Only the named approver can decide. Every decision is logged.Approved: the agent can release. Rejected: back to draft with the comment.
7
Release and email it
S09 · QuotationS33 · Email templates
Clicks Release, then Email. The email uses the quotation template with the PDF attached.A released quotation is locked. Valid-until date is required.Released. The list warns 3 days before it expires.
8
Customer accepts?
—
Customer replies by email, phone or a signed copy.—Accepted → sales order. Not accepted → revise or lost.
9
Revise, or mark as lost
S08 · Quotation list
Revise makes revision 1 under the same number. Lost asks for a reason.Only released quotations can be revised. An expired one can be revised with a new date.Revision goes back to step 3. Lost closes the enquiry as Lost.
10
Turn it into a sales order
S11 · Sales order
Clicks Transfer to sales order. All lines, or only some, are copied.Prices and tax are copied as quoted. The credit check runs when the order is saved (F2).SO number, Draft. Quotation → Ordered.
