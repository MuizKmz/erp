Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Create the RFQ and pick suppliers
P07 · RFQ listP08 · Compare replies
From the request, clicks Ask suppliers (RFQ). Picks suppliers from Master Data and sets a reply-by date.At least 2 suppliers when the order is over RM 50,000.00. Only active suppliers can be asked.RFQ number, status Waiting.
2
Each supplier gets an email and reply link
P26 · Email templates
Nothing to click. Each supplier gets the RFQ request email with its own reply link.Template: RFQ request (P26).The list shows replies as dots: 0 of 2.
3
Reply with price, delivery time and terms
—
Supplier enters price, delivery time, minimum order, payment term and validity, and can attach their quote PDF.—A reply dot fills in on the RFQ list.
4
Enough replies by the reply date?
—
Buyer watches the reply count and uses Remind for late suppliers.Comparing can start before every reply is in; a missing supplier shows as not replied.—
5
Compare replies side by side
P08 · Compare replies
Opens the compare view: suppliers side by side, lowest price and fastest delivery highlighted, plus each supplier’s on-time record.On-time record comes from past goods receipts.—
6
Any offer acceptable?
—
Buyer judges if any offer is acceptable.—No: the RFQ closes without an order and suppliers are told.
7
Award, with a reason if not the cheapest
P08 · Compare replies
Picks a supplier and clicks Award and create PO. If it is not the cheapest, types the reason.A reason is required when the cheapest is not chosen. The approver sees it and it stays in the audit trail.RFQ → Awarded.
8
Draft PO created; others told “not this time”
—
Nothing to click.Price, lead time and payment term are copied from the winning reply.Draft PO; the request closes. Example: RFQ-0001 → PO-0001.
