Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Check lines, delivery date and terms
P10 · Purchase order
Opens the draft PO. Supplier, lines and price come from the RFQ, the request or the sales order. Sets expected delivery and the receiving warehouse.Buy-to-order POs keep the link to the sales order. Service POs need no goods receipt (P25).—
2
Over the branch limit?
—
The screen compares the total with the approval limit of the branch.HQ over RM 10,000.00 → Purchasing Manager. Penang over RM 5,000.00 → Branch Manager (P24).Under the limit: can be released at once.
3
Send for approval with a note
P11 · Send for approval
Clicks Send for approval. A dialog explains why, names the approver, summarises the order and asks for a note.The PO is locked while it waits.Pending approval. The approver gets an email and an Approvals item.
4
Approve or reject
P02 · Approvals inbox
Approver opens it in Approvals and decides with a comment.The buyer can never approve their own PO.Approve: can be released. Reject: back to draft with the comment.
5
Second approval, only over RM 50,000
P02 · Approvals inbox
Finance Director approves as a second step.Only for orders over RM 50,000.00.Fully approved.
6
Release and email to the supplier
P10 · Purchase orderP26 · Email templates
Clicks Release. The PO is emailed to the supplier with the PDF.“Send automatically on release” can be turned off in Email Templates (P26).Released. Header and lines lock.
7
Confirms the delivery date
—
Supplier replies to confirm the delivery date.—Buyer updates the expected date if it changed.
8
Committed spend updated
—
Nothing to click.Committed spend counts released POs only.The PO shows under “waiting to be received”.
