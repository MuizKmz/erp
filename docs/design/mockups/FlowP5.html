Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Record the supplier invoice
P16 · Invoice list
Opens Purchase Invoice, clicks Record supplier invoice, picks the supplier and PO, enters their invoice number and date, and attaches the PDF.The same supplier invoice number cannot be recorded twice.PI number, To match.
2
Three-way match runs
P17 · Three-way match
The match table fills itself from the PO and the goods receipts: quantity, unit price, amount and tax side by side, with a result per line.Billing is matched against received quantity, never ordered.Each line shows ✓ or ✗ with the difference.
3
Within tolerance?
—
—Tolerance from Approval Rules (P24): price ±2%, quantity 0 over received, tax ±RM 1.00.Inside tolerance: goes straight to payable.
4
What should we record?
—
Three choices are shown with their effect in plain words.——
5
Record only what was received
P17 · Three-way match
Picks Record only what was received (PI-0001: 95 of 100 rolls, RM 4,180.00) and sends the prefilled message.Goods not received are never paid for until they arrive.Approved for the received amount; supplier emailed.
6
Approve the difference
P02 · Approvals inbox
Manager sees the difference and the supplier’s note in Approvals (PI-0002: freight added, +3.2%).Needed whenever an invoice is accepted outside tolerance.Approved: recorded as billed.
7
Sends a corrected invoice or credit note
—
Supplier sends a corrected invoice or a credit note.The invoice stays on hold meanwhile.Recorded again from step 1.
8
Payable queued for Finance
—
A banner says Finance is not connected yet.Approved invoices are kept as payables.Posted automatically when Finance is plugged in.
9
Finance pays by the due date
—
Nothing to click in Purchase.Due date comes from the supplier’s payment term.Invoice Paid.
