Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Credit check runs on save
S29 · Credit control
Nothing to click. The check runs when the agent saves or releases.Rules per document come from Credit Control (S29): allow with a warning, require approval, or block. The limit is group-wide or per branch.Pass: release is allowed. Fail: release is blocked for now.
3
Ask for approval with a reason
S12 · Credit check failed
A red panel shows limit, owed now, this order and how much it is over. The agent types a reason and clicks Request approval.Release stays blocked until approved. Cancelling the order withdraws the request.The request appears in the Sales Manager’s Approvals inbox.
4
Approve or reject
S02 · Approvals inbox
Manager sees exposure, overdue amount, last payment and the agent’s reason. Approves or rejects with a comment.The requester can never approve their own order.Approve: the agent can release. Reject: the order stays draft with the comment.
5
Release the order
S11 · Sales order
Clicks Release. Header and lines lock.Short stock only warns. After release, changes need a revision or a cancel of the balance (F6).Released. The order shows in the delivery waiting list.
6
Order released event
—
Nothing to click.Event sales.order.released is saved even when Inventory or Purchase are not connected.Buy-to-order lines: Purchase drafts a PO, like PO-0003 for SO-0003.
7
Create delivery order
S15 · Delivery orders
Store opens Delivery orders, sees orders waiting with balance and stock, and clicks Create DO.Only released orders with a balance appear.DO number, Draft.
8
Confirm what left the warehouse
S16 · Confirm delivery
Enters vehicle, driver, consignment note and quantity delivered now; uploads the signed DO. Clicks Confirm delivery.Deliver now cannot be more than the balance. A confirmed DO is locked; mistakes are fixed with a return.Balance drops (100 → 40), status Partial or Done. Delivered qty can now be invoiced.
9
Delivery event saved for Inventory
—
Nothing to click. A banner says Inventory is not connected yet.Event sales.shipment.requested waits until Inventory is plugged in.When Inventory is connected, picking and delivery move there.
