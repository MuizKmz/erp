Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Create proforma from the order
S22 · Proforma
Opens Proforma Invoice, clicks New proforma and picks the order. Chooses deposit %, or pay before release, and a pay-by date.A proforma is only a payment request: nothing is posted, nothing goes to LHDN, and the order balance does not change.PF number, Draft.
2
Release and email it
S21 · Proforma listS33 · Email templates
Clicks Release, then Email. Bank details print from Master Data.—Sent. The delivery list shows the order waiting for Deposit · PF-0004.
4
Remind, or cancel the order (F6)
S21 · Proforma list
The agent sees the proforma still unpaid in the list and follows up.Delivery stays on hold while the deposit is unpaid.Customer pays, or the order balance is cancelled (F6).
5
Finance records the deposit
—
Nothing to click in Sales.Receipts are recorded in Finance against the proforma number.Proforma → Paid. The deposit is held for the invoice.
6
Deliver the order
S16 · Confirm delivery
Store delivers as in F2.—Delivered quantity can be invoiced.
7
Invoice what was delivered
S18 · Create invoiceS19 · Invoice
Billing creates the invoice as in F3.Only the tax invoice goes to LHDN, never the proforma.Invoice released.
8
Deposit used on the invoice
—
The invoice shows the deposit taken from the proforma.A deposit can be used only once.Proforma → Used on INV-xxxx. Balance due = total minus deposit.
