Rules

Seven rules users can rely on

1Goods can never be received beyond the open PO quantity.
2A bill is never paid for goods that have not arrived.
3The buyer can never approve their own PO.
4Orders over RM 50,000.00 need replies from at least 2 suppliers, unless the item has a contract price.
5Choosing a supplier that is not the cheapest needs a written reason.
6Only active suppliers from Master Data can be used.
7Released documents are locked. Fix them with a cancel order, a purchase return or a debit note.

Pattern 5

Working with other modules

Inventory is not connected yet. This receipt is saved and the stock movement is queued.
