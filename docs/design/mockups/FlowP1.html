Step by step

Numbers match the boxes above
Step · screenWhat the user does and seesRules the screen enforcesWhat changes after
1
Check the demand list
P04 · Demand
Opens Demand. Each line says why it needs buying (below reorder, or for a sales order), what is on hand and on order, and a suggested quantity.Suggested qty tops stock up to the reorder level, adds what open sales orders still need, and rounds up to the pack size. Lines already covered by an open PO are greyed out.Selected lines are ready to become a request.
2
Raise and submit a request
P05 · Request listP06 · Purchase request
Ticks lines and clicks Raise request for selected, or clicks Add new request for anything not on the list, like printer toner. Adds a needed-by date and reason, then submits.Items come from Master Data. Needed-by date and reason are required to submit.PRQ number, status Submitted. The buyer is notified.
3
Review the request
P06 · Purchase request
Buyer opens the request. The right panel shows stock today, usage in the last 30 days, open sales orders using the item and the last three prices paid.Preferred supplier and last price come from Master Data and purchase history.—
4
Needed and justified?
—
Buyer decides whether it should be bought now.Rejecting needs a reason, which the requester sees.Rejected requests stay on the list for audit.
5
Price known and no RFQ needed?
—
Buyer checks the value and whether the item has a contract price.Orders over RM 50,000.00 need an RFQ with at least 2 suppliers, unless the item has a contract price (Approval Rules, P24).—
6
Create the PO directly
P10 · Purchase order
Clicks Create PO directly. Supplier, item, quantity and last price are copied into a draft PO.Only active suppliers from Master Data can be used.Request → Converted, linked to the PO.
7
Purchase drafts the PO automatically
—
Nothing to click. When Sales releases an order with a buy-to-order line, a draft PO appears for the buyer.Rule “Auto-draft a PO for buy-to-order sales lines” is on (P24). The PO keeps the link to the sales order.Example: SO-0003 released → PO-0003 drafted for 20 sofas.
