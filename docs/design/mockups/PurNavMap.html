Home tiles

Purchase home has three tiles, one per menu group, plus the dashboard with open POs, goods to receive and invoices to match.

Numbers are links

Click any document number to open it. PO lines link to their goods receipts and invoices.

Document flow

From a PO, one click shows request, RFQ, receipts, invoices and returns, with what is still open.
