Staff asks if the buyer needs their own e-Invoice.—Most walk-in buyers do not.

S24 · Cash sale
Types the buyer’s name, phone and TIN.A TIN is required for an own e-Invoice.This sale gets its own e-Invoice.
5
Take payment and release
S24 · Cash sale
Picks the payment method and enters the amount received or card reference; change is calculated. Clicks Take payment and release.Balance must be 0 before release.Receipt prints. Sale and payment recorded in one document.
6
Sale, payment and stock-out recorded
—
Nothing to click.Stock-out event saved for Inventory.Cash sales list and today’s counter total update.

Own e-Invoice now, or in the monthly consolidated one

Nothing to click.Sales without their own e-Invoice go into the monthly consolidated e-Invoice, due by the 7th of next month.Sent when the e-Invoice module is plugged in.
